Active Directory DS / PowerShell

Enforcing Least Privilege in a Finance-to-IT Transfer

An internal transfer risked carrying sensitive Finance permissions into a new IT role. I audited and revoked legacy group memberships before assigning new role-based access to prevent privilege creep.

How I verified the transfer

I removed the Finance group membership, added the IT group, updated the department, and moved the account into the IT organizational unit. I checked both group memberships and the account location after the move.
